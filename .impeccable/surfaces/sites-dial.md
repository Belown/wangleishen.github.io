# Surface brief: sites/dial (home + section + work pages)

Mode: Experience. Personal site of one maker (research, design & engineering, art/music/photo, writing). Visitors: the owner and friends. First viewport leads with a short self-introduction (user's answer), then direct navigation to all five sections. User rejected: colour/type that jumps, indirect navigation, whole-site scene metaphors. User steer: restrained but not plain; Apple-level "suppressed beauty" and premium finish. Bilingual zh/en first-class. All content is placeholder (Ada Lin) and stays labelled as such.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Premium through finishing, not ornament: the site is built like a Swiss dial is finished, sunray-brushed ground, applied polished markers, hairline type. Refuses the hero-plus-card-grid portfolio and refuses a literal watch illustration: no hands, no numerals ring, no clock face.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-blue sunray ground (#141c2b to #2a3a55) whose radial brushing is barely visible; applied markers as small polished bars with a light edge; hairline chapter rings as rules; silver-white text (#d8dde3); one lume colour (#e9e2c9) that means "current" and nothing else. Depth only from stacked layers (ground, ring, applied marker), never blur shadows. One central axis; everything symmetric or aligned to it.

STORY: The visitor reads who this is in one sentence, sees all five sections at once as markers, picks one, and moves through works one held view at a time; each work's state is engraved on the surface ("3 / 24", date, medium).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport dial ground. Centre: name very large and very light (zh), Latin name tracked wide beneath, one-line intro. Bottom edge: five section markers in a row, each an applied bar over its label, active one in lume. Top: one engraved line (place, year, work count). Language switch engraved top-right.

FORM: Dial Finishing, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned). seed:bd5aede07c894f8d

Signature interaction: the light. Pointer/scroll shifts the sunray brushing's highlight angle very slightly, like tilting a dial under a lamp (reduced-motion: static). Transitions between views are clean cuts with a short fade, no sliding.

Raises: depth by stacked layers only (papercut); one held composition per view, cuts not slides (wuxia); one central axis (synth); lume means only "current" (crt); state engraved on the surface, no badges (terminal).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
