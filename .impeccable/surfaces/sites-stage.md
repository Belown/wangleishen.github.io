# Surface brief: the site (repo root, formerly sites/stage) (home + section + work pages)

Mode: Experience. Personal site of one maker (research, design & engineering, art/music/photo, writing). Visitors: the owner, friends, and (since 2026-11-24) HR and prospective supervisors. First viewport leads with a short self-introduction (user's answer), with direct navigation to all five sections. User rejected: colour/type that jumps, indirect navigation, whole-site scene metaphors. User steer: restrained but not plain; Apple-level "suppressed beauty" and premium finish. Bilingual zh/en first-class. All content is placeholder (Ada Lin) and stays labelled as such.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One work in the light, everything else in reach. The site is a dark stage: the first screen puts one work in a single warm top light with the name; below it, the room is lit for reading (a full self-introduction for recruiters and advisors, then a dense works index). Refuses the thumbnail grid and the busy portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black stage (#0b0b0c) with a deeper floor (#1c1c1f); one soft warm top light (radial falloff, never a hard spotlight cone); text in warm white (#f2f2f0) and grey (#9a9aa0) only; no accent colour; the light itself is the accent. Work media sits in the light with a real contact shadow on the floor. Large confident display type, tight but not cramped; small quiet captions.

STORY: The visitor sees the name and the newest work in the light; scrolling down, a recruiter or advisor gets the self-introduction (bio and research interests, education and experience, publications and honours, contact and CV PDF), then a compact index of all works; sections filter the index; a work page is a full case study under the same light.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Concise. Left/top: name in large display type and one short line. Centre-right: the newest work's image standing in the light with its floor shadow, caption (title · year · section) beneath. Bottom: a quiet row of the five sections plus language switch.

FORM: Night Stage, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the user alongside the roll). seed:bd5aede07c894f8d

Signature interaction: the light. On the first screen the light visibly lands on the work (catch-light on its top edge, lit floor pool); as the visitor scrolls past, the light swells once and settles, and the work dims as the introduction rises. Reduced-motion: static, still lit.

Revision 2026-11-24 (owner feedback): first screen concise, self-introduction on scroll for HR/advisors; layout and sample content were too sparse, so density goes up below the first screen.
