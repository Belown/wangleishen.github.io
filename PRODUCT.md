# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primarily the owner and their friends. The site is personal expression first: a place to keep and show what the owner makes, and for friends to browse it. Admissions committees, employers and collaborators are not the target; any of them may visit, but nothing should be optimised for them at the expense of the personal character.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that holds everything the owner makes in one place: academic research (papers, projects), design and engineering work, art, music and photography, and writing. Success means the owner is happy to keep adding to it, and friends enjoy wandering through it.

## Positioning

One person's whole practice in one place, research next to music next to essays, without ranking one above the others. The breadth is the point. (Inferred from the owner's chosen content areas and the reference site they admired; confirm before treating as binding.)

## Operating Context

- Content is bilingual: Chinese and English, with the main content available in both and a way to switch.
- The owner will keep adding work over time, so adding a project, post, photo or recording must stay easy.
- Existing build: a Vite + React + TypeScript app in this folder (`src/`), with placeholder content in `src/content/` and a drop-in asset folder at `src/assets/`. A second experimental copy lives in `bogujia/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: research, design and engineering, art / music / photography, writing (journal), about.
- The owner found 3D scenes hard to build and maintain and wants to move to pure 2D design.
- Undecided: real name, bio and content. All current content (the persona "Ada Lin", projects, posts) is placeholder and must not be presented as real.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No real projects, papers, photos, recordings or texts have been provided. Do not invent publications, awards or biography presented as the owner's.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the site is the room it hangs in.
2. Breadth without hierarchy: research, making, art and writing sit side by side.
3. Easy to keep alive: adding something new should take minutes, not a redesign.
4. Both languages are first-class, not a translation afterthought.
