The aria never changes, but I do. Some mornings it is too fast because I am anxious. Some mornings the left hand is louder because I slept badly.

Design reviews could work like this. Repeat the same small thing daily and notice what changed in you, not only in the work.

After four hundred mornings the aria is not finished. I think that is the point.
