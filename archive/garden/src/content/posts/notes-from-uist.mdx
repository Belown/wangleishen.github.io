The best demos were the quiet ones. A glove that only vibrated when you were about to drop something. A chair that leaned back a few degrees when your breathing slowed.

When Hum Atlas won, the first thing I felt was relief that the prototype had survived the flight. The second was a strong wish to go home and sleep.

I came back with a notebook full of questions and one rule for next year: bring fewer slides and more objects.
